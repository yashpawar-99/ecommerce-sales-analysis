# Conclusion

## 1. Sales

- Technology generated the highest sales and contributed approximately 36% of total company sales.
- Phones and Chairs were the top-performing sub-categories, with very similar total sales.
- West generated the highest total sales and had the highest number of unique orders among all regions.
- East had the highest average revenue per order among the regions.

## 2. Customer

- The top 10 customers contributed approximately 6.8% of total company sales.
- Approximately 98.36% of customers were repeat customers.
- The Consumer segment generated approximately 50.8% of total company sales.
- The Consumer segment had the highest number of unique customers, with 409 customers.
- Corporate customers had the highest average revenue per customer.

## 3. Time

- 2017 had the highest year-over-year sales growth at approximately 30.64%.
- Sales declined by approximately 4.26% in 2016 compared with 2015.
- Sales increased by approximately 20.30% in 2018 compared with 2017.
- November 2018 was the highest-sales month, generating approximately $117.9K in sales.

## Business Recommendations

### Based on the analysis:

- Focus on high-performing Technology products and identify opportunities to increase their contribution further.
- Investigate why the West region generates the highest total sales and identify strategies that could be replicated in other regions.
- Develop strategies to acquire and retain more high-value Corporate customers.
- Investigate the factors behind the strong sales growth observed in 2017 and 2018.
- Analyze high-performing months such as November 2018 to understand potential seasonal or promotional effects.
